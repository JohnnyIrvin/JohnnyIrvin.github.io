I build software, and I break it.

In the beginning, I began as a software developer and architect. At Point3 Security, I co-led the development of ESCALATE, a cybersecurity training platform. I helped redesign it from a static site with a simple Flask backend into a single-page app built on GraphQL and microservices. Later, I would lead another team in building a cyber range for the U.S. Armed Forces.

At Zimperium, I kept a legacy platform secure and FedRAMP-compliant. I reproduced n-day vulnerabilities and helped with remediation. That’s where I shifted from building software to breaking it.

For the past few years, I’ve been researching zero-day vulnerabilities in mobile and web applications. In 2026, I received the Booz Allen Hamilton’s VIP Award for my discoveries.

I know how attackers think and how engineers work, so I can identify what matters and get it fixed without slowing teams down.
